# Female - Anus

## Purpose

This node defines the anus as a canonical anatomical site within Female Embodiment.

It exists to provide a clear anatomical anchor for pelvic, perineal, boundary, mirror, fluid-adjacent, activation, sensory, symbolic, authorial, and corpus traversal without collapsing those downstream layers into the canonical node.

---

## Canonical Definition

Female - Anus is the external terminal opening of the anal canal, located in the posterior perineal region.

It is anatomically distinct from the vulva, vaginal opening, urethral opening, perineal boundary, and pelvic floor, while remaining adjacent to pelvic and perineal structures.

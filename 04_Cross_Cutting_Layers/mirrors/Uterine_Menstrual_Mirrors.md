# Uterine & Menstrual Mirrors (Female)

**Type:** Mirror (Cross-Cutting)  
**Primary Embodiment:** Female

## Triggering Activation

Common activations that generate these mirror responses:

- Menstrual cramps and uterine contractions
- Hormonal shifts across the cycle
- Bleeding and blood awareness
- Emotional states tied to cyclical change

## Mirror Mapping

| Origin                        | Mirror Response                          | Affected Areas / Registers          |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Uterine contractions          | Lower back, pelvic floor, thighs         | Somatic                             |
| Menstrual pain                | Fatigue, emotional sensitivity, withdrawal | Somatic, Emotional                |
| Blood awareness               | Shame, power, or ritual significance     | Emotional, Symbolic, Liturgical     |
| Hormonal shifts               | Mood, energy, and sensory sensitivity    | Emotional, Sensory                  |

## Cross-Cutting Behavior

- Strong connection to Shared states (fatigue, emotional sensitivity, withdrawal)
- Often produces cyclical or wave-like mirror patterns
- Can create powerful ritual or symbolic resonance when witnessed

## Notes

- One of the most cyclical and time-sensitive mirror systems
- Works especially well in scenes involving menstruation, fertility awareness, or hormonal intensity
- Requires sensitivity around cultural and personal meanings of blood and cycles

**Status:** Draft v1

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the layered architecture and relational logic of the K.S. McCrae Ontology. It establishes the structure, rules, and governance required to evolve the existing V2 Lexicon into a composable, traversable ontological system while protecting the author fingerprint and sacred constraints.

It serves as both documentation and a **revision gate** for future development.

---

## 2. Core Principles

- **Equal Transition**: All transitions from the Master Embodiment Layers must be applied equally across registers.
- **Limited Sharing**: Level 1 Cross-Cutting Layers (Fluids, Pairs, Mirrors, Propagation) have controlled sharing. Most expressions remain embodiment-specific.
- **Bi-directional Activation**: The relationship between Master Embodiment Layers and Tropes is bi-directional.
- **Cross-Cutting Mirrors**: Mirrors operate across multiple layers rather than being confined to a single category.
- **Variable Influence**: Systems under Systemic act as influencers whose strength varies depending on context.
- **Temporal Modulation**: The Temporal register affects and modulates other expressive registers.
- **Fingerprint Protection**: All layers and transitions must preserve implication, somatic priority, and authorial voice.

---

## 3. Layer Model Overview

The ontology is organized into four levels:

- **Level 0**: Primary Activation Drivers
- **Level 1**: Cross-Cutting Translation Layers
- **Level 2**: Core Expressive Registers
- **Level 3**: Voice & Authorship Systems

---

## 4. Level 0 – Primary Activation Drivers

These two elements sit at the root and jointly determine what activates in the layers below.

### Master Embodiment Layers
- **female**
- **male**
- **shared**
- **trans_feminine**
- **trans_masculine**

Each layer carries its own anatomical, physiological, hormonal, and experiential foundations.

### Tropes
Tropes function as **selection and activation logic**, not as expression. They determine which embodiment pathways, relationship structures, and expressive registers receive emphasis.

**Relationship Note**: The connection between Master Embodiment Layers and Tropes is **bi-directional**.

---

## 5. Level 1 – Cross-Cutting Translation Layers

These layers move meaning between embodiment, environment, systems, and expression. Sharing across embodiment layers is limited.

### Fluids
- Female-specific fluids
- Male-specific fluids
- Trans-specific fluids
- Shared fluids (e.g. Saliva, Blood, Sweat, Tears, Mucus)

### Relational
- Pairs
- Witness
- Power

### Mirrors
Mirrors capture cross-body sympathetic responses. They are **cross-cutting** and can operate across Fluids, Propagation, and Core Expressive Registers.

### Propagation
- Internal Propagation
- Relational Propagation
- Environmental Propagation
- Systemic Propagation

---

## 6. Level 2 – Core Expressive Registers

These registers are where meaning becomes lived experience and expression.

- **Sensory** – Direct perception
- **Somatic** – Body response
- **Emotional** – Internal feeling states
- **Environmental** – Environment as active participant
- **Sonic** – Sound as meaning
- **Symbolic** – Meaning beyond action
- **Liturgical** – Ritual and ceremony
- **Instrument** – Transformational tools
- **Systemic** (Container)
  - Ethical Dignity System
  - Optimization Systems
  - Environmental Systems
  - Story Operating Systems
- **Temporal** – Meaning created through time; modulates other registers

---

## 7. Level 3 – Voice & Authorship Systems

These systems protect authorship and prevent architectural drift.

### Example Register
Classified excerpts from published work with metadata (System Type, Trope, Densities, etc.).

### Human Drift Register
Captures controlled imperfection:
- Cadence variation
- Structural asymmetry
- Controlled omission
- Imperfect observation
- Selective incompleteness

---

## 8. Key Relational Rules & Governance

These rules function as the **revision gate** for the ontology:

- Transitions from Master Embodiment Layers must be applied equally.
- The Embodiment ↔ Trope relationship is bi-directional.
- Mirrors are cross-cutting.
- Temporal modulates other registers in Level 2.
- Systems under Systemic act as variable influencers.
- Story Operating Systems maintains a strong relationship with Level 3.
- Limited sharing applies in Level 1.
- All changes must preserve somatic priority, implication, and authorial voice.

---

## 9. Core Flow

**Embodiment + Trope** → **Level 1 Translation Layers** → **Level 2 Expressive Registers** → **Level 3 Voice Systems** → Story

---

## 10. Governance & Revision Gate

This specification serves as the reference point for all future development. Any proposed changes to layer structure, relationships, or core rules must be evaluated against the principles and logic documented in Sections 2 and 8.

Significant changes should result in a new version of this specification.

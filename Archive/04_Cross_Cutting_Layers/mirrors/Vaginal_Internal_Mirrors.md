# Vaginal & Internal Mirrors (Female)

**Type:** Mirror (Cross-Cutting)  
**Primary Embodiment:** Female

## Triggering Activation

Common activations that generate these mirror responses:

- Vaginal penetration or pressure
- Internal touch and sensation
- Cervical stimulation
- Fullness and stretching

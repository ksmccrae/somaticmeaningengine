**Primary Embodiment:** Female (with reach into Shared and others)

## Triggering Activation

Common activations that generate cross-embodiment mirror responses from female states:

- Breast/nipple stimulation and lactation
- Menstrual and uterine states
- Clitoral and orgasmic states
- Vulnerability, exposure, and cyclical emotional shifts

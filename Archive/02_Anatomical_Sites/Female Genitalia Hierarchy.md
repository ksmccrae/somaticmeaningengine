# Design Principles

This hierarchy is intended for ontology construction rather than medical education.

The hierarchy separates:
- **Composite Nodes** — containers or organizational structures
- **Atomic Nodes** — discrete anatomical structures
- **Functional Nodes** — experiential, somatic, or propagative systems (treated as tags/concepts within Ontology Metadata at this stage)

---

# Level 1: Composite Nodes

## Vulva
**Type:** Composite Anatomical Node

The vulva represents the external female genitalia as a whole. It serves as the primary external genital composite structure.

**Children:**
- Mons Pubis
- Labia Majora
- Labia Minora
- Clitoral Complex
- Vestibule

**Ontology Notes:**
- High symbolic importance
- High sensory significance
- Strong relational and witness functions
- Often acts as an activation gateway into deeper anatomical systems

## Clitoral Complex
**Type:** Composite Anatomical Node

The complete erectile clitoral system. Modern anatomical understanding groups the glans, body, crura, and vestibular bulbs together as a distributed erectile network.

**Children:**
- Clitoral Glans
- Clitoral Hood
- Clitoral Body
- Clitoral Crura
- Vestibular Bulbs

**Ontology Notes:**
- Primary erotic sensory structure in female embodiment
- Strong Mirror Hub and Propagation Hub roles
- High salience within Female register

## Vestibule
**Type:** Composite Anatomical Node (light)

The space enclosed by the labia minora containing several anatomical openings.

**Children:**
- Urethral Opening
- Vaginal Opening

**Ontology Notes:**
- Functions as a transition zone between external and internal anatomy
- Kept as a lighter composite for organizational clarity

# Level 2: Atomic Nodes

## Mons Pubis
**Type:** Atomic Anatomical Node

Fatty tissue overlying the pubic symphysis.

**Functions:** Cushioning, protection, visual/aesthetic role.

## Labia Majora
**Type:** Atomic Anatomical Node

Outer folds of the vulva.

**Functions:** Protection, containment, external sensory response.

## Labia Minora
**Type:** Atomic Anatomical Node

Inner folds surrounding the vestibule.

**Functions:** Protection, sensory response, lubrication support.

**Mirror Potential:** Strong connection to clitoral structures and pelvic floor.

## Clitoral Glans
**Type:** Atomic Anatomical Node

External visible portion of the clitoris.

**Functions:** Dense sensory innervation, direct tactile responsiveness.

## Clitoral Hood
**Type:** Atomic Anatomical Node

Protective fold surrounding the clitoral glans.

**Functions:** Protection, friction modulation.

**Witness Characteristics:** Strong concealment/revelation dynamics.

## Clitoral Body
**Type:** Atomic Anatomical Node

Internal shaft of the clitoris.

**Functions:** Erectile response, internal propagation.

## Clitoral Crura
**Type:** Atomic Anatomical Node

Paired erectile structures extending along the pubic arch.

**Functions:** Erectile support, deep activation pathways.

## Vestibular Bulbs
**Type:** Atomic Anatomical Node

Paired erectile tissue structures beneath the vestibule (part of the Clitoral Complex).

**Functions:** Erectile engorgement, pressure response, somatic amplification.

**Mirror Potential:** Clitoral body, labia minora, pelvic floor.

## Urethral Opening
**Type:** Atomic Anatomical Node

External opening of the urethra.

## Vaginal Opening
**Type:** Atomic Anatomical Node

External entrance to the vaginal canal.

# Trans Feminine

**Type:** Master Embodiment Layer (Level 0)  
**Status:** Active

## Purpose

The Trans Feminine layer represents the embodied experience of those who identify and live as feminine, including individuals who have undergone various forms of medical, social, and hormonal transition. It honors both the lived feminine experience and the specific realities of transition.

## Core Dimensions

### Anatomy & Physiology
- Post-surgical anatomy (vaginoplasty, breast augmentation, facial feminization, etc.)
- Hormone replacement therapy (estrogen, anti-androgens)
- Breast development from HRT
- Altered genital and pelvic sensation
- Skin, fat distribution, and hair pattern changes

### Hormonal & Reproductive States
- Estrogen-dominant hormonal profiles (exogenous)
- Effects of testosterone suppression
- Absence of menstrual cycles (in most cases)
- Potential for lactation in some individuals after long-term HRT

### Embodied Experience
- Breast and nipple sensitivity from HRT
- Changed skin texture and sensitivity
- Altered genital and orgasmic experience
- Shift in fat distribution and body shape
- Gender euphoria and dysphoria as somatic experiences

## Shared Elements

Elements that overlap with other embodiment layers:

- Skin, breath, temperature, touch, fatigue, pain, hunger, ageing, mortality
- Emotional and nervous system responses

## Specific Considerations

Unique aspects of this embodiment layer:

- Embodied experience of medical transition and its ongoing effects
- Navigation of both assigned and affirmed embodiment
- Specific post-surgical and HRT-related sensations
- Often carries complex relationships with blood, fertility, and "female" bodily expectations

## Relationship to Tropes

How this embodiment layer typically interacts with or selects tropes:

- Tropes involving transition, medicalization, or transformation often engage this layer directly
- Some tropes take on additional layers of meaning around authenticity, passing, or medical intervention
- Lactation and breast-centered tropes can have particular resonance here

## Mirror Dynamics

How this layer generates or receives mirror responses:

- HRT and post-surgical states can create unique mirror patterns
- Breast development and sensitivity often produce strong cross-embodiment resonance
- Gendered bodily experiences can create complex mirror dynamics with both cis and other trans embodiments

## Notes

- This layer must be handled with care, respect, and precision
- Avoid reducing trans feminine experience to medical transition alone
- Honor both the shared feminine experience and the distinct realities of transition

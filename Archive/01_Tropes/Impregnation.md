# Impregnation

**Type:** Trope (Level 0)  
**Function:** Selection & Activation Logic

## Purpose

The Impregnation trope focuses on the act, fantasy, or consequence of being impregnated or impregnating another. It centers on conception, filling, fertility, and the moment of becoming pregnant.

## Activation Logic

What this trope tends to emphasize or bring forward:

- Embodiment pathways: Internal sensation of being filled, conception, quickening, and early pregnancy
- Relationship structures: Breeder/breeder, inseminator/receiver, claimer/claimed
- Expressive registers: Claiming, surrender, fertility, risk, and transformation

## Common Embodiment Interactions

| Embodiment Layer   | Typical Expression / Emphasis                                      |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Female             | Being impregnated, breeding kink, fertility play, risk of pregnancy|
| Male               | Impregnating, breeding, claiming through insemination              |
| Trans Feminine     | Impregnation play (often fantasy or through specific configurations)|
| Trans Masculine    | Impregnation themes (often complex or fantasy-based)               |
| Shared             | General fertility and conception themes                            |

## Relational Dynamics

Common pair/power/witness configurations associated with this trope:

- Breeder / Receiver
- Inseminator / Inseminated
- Claimer / Claimed
- Risk-aware conception play

## Propagation Patterns

Typical ways this trope moves through systems:

- Filling → conception → transformation → consequence
- Claiming → surrender → identity shift

## Mirror Effects

Common sympathetic or cross-body responses associated with this trope:

- Impregnation often produces strong mirror responses around claiming and surrender
- Fertility and risk can create intense resonance across embodiments
- The theme of being filled and changed resonates widely

## Notes / Constraints

- This trope requires clear consent and risk awareness
- Strong potential for both erotic charge and emotional intensity
- Works best when grounded in embodiment rather than pure fantasy

## Example Usage

- Breeding and impregnation scenes
- Risk and fertility play
- Claiming through insemination
- Conception as transformation or consequence

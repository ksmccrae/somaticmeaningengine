# Breeding

**Type:** Trope (Level 0)  
**Function:** Selection & Activation Logic

## Purpose

The Breeding trope encompasses ongoing themes of fertility, repeated insemination, ownership, and the use of a body for reproductive purposes. It often includes power exchange, status, and long-term consequences.

## Activation Logic

What this trope tends to emphasize or bring forward:

- Embodiment pathways: Repeated filling, fertility maintenance, ownership of reproductive capacity
- Relationship structures: Breeder/breeder, owner/breeding stock, program/participant
- Expressive registers: Ownership, use, fertility, status, and long-term consequence

## Common Embodiment Interactions

| Embodiment Layer   | Typical Expression / Emphasis                                      |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Female             | Ongoing breeding, breeding programs, repeated insemination         |
| Male               | Breeding others, maintaining breeding stock                        |
| Trans Feminine     | Breeding themes in specific power exchange contexts                |
| Trans Masculine    | Breeding themes in specific power exchange contexts                |
| Shared             | General fertility and reproductive use themes                      |

## Relational Dynamics

Common pair/power/witness configurations associated with this trope:

- Owner / Breeding stock
- Breeder / Program participant
- Handler / Stock
- Long-term breeding arrangements

## Propagation Patterns

Typical ways this trope moves through systems:

- Repeated use → fertility maintenance → status and ownership → long-term consequence
- Claiming → ongoing use → identity as breeding resource

## Mirror Effects

Common sympathetic or cross-body responses associated with this trope:

- Breeding often produces strong mirror responses around ownership and use
- Repeated fertility themes can create deep resonance
- The ongoing nature of the trope can induce trance or identity shift

## Notes / Constraints

- This trope requires very clear ongoing consent and aftercare
- Strong potential for both erotic intensity and emotional weight
- Works best when balanced with care and precision

## Example Usage

- Long-term breeding and ownership dynamics
- Breeding programs and fertility maintenance
- Repeated insemination as power exchange
- Fertility status and consequence themes

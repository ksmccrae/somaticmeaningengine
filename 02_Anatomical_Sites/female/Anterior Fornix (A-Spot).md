## Purpose

The anterior fornix erogenous zone (commonly called the A-spot or AFE zone) is located deep on the anterior (front) vaginal wall, near the cervix. It is reported by some individuals to be a site of deep internal pleasure, lubrication response, and sometimes orgasm when stimulated.

- May trigger significant lubrication in some individuals

- Located roughly 4–6 inches inside on the front wall

# Trans Feminine

**Type:** Master Embodiment Layer

## Overview / Core Essence

The Trans Feminine embodiment layer encompasses the embodied experience of individuals whose gender identity aligns with femininity, including those who have undergone or are undergoing medical and social transition.

Common patterns of internal bodily awareness:

- Hormonal shifts and emotional changes
- Post-surgical sensation changes
- Gender euphoria / dysphoria patterns

## Governance Considerations

The Trans Feminine layer must center autonomy, consent, and self-definition. It should never be reduced to medical status or "before/after" narratives.

## Relationship to Story Systems

Trans Feminine embodiment often interacts powerfully with Ethical Dignity Systems and may challenge or expand Resonance and Transformation systems.

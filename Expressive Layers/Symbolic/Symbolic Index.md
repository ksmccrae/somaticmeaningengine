# Symbolic Index

The Symbolic sublayer defines interpretation, significance, archetype, motif, or symbolic meaning assigned downstream from embodied or expressive material.

Symbolic records do not define objective biology, activation mechanics, sensation itself, somatic body-state, valence, desire, authorial wording, corpus truth, or consent.

## Boundary Pattern

```text
Activation
= physiological process or state change

Sensory
= perceived qualities

Somatic
= body-state or embodied orientation

Emotional / Motivational
= affective or motivational orientation

Symbolic
= interpretation or significance

Authorial
= language and style

Corpus
= situated use in a specific work
```

Symbolic meaning may be assigned downstream, but it must not overwrite the upstream biological or expressive records.

## Shared-Term Governance

Terms such as Containment and Release may appear in multiple layers.

Their meaning must remain typed by the record using them:

```text
Somatic Containment / Release
= body-state

Symbolic Containment / Release
= interpretation or significance
```

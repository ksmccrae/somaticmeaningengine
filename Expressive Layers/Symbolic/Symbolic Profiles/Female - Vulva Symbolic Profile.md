# Female - Vulva Symbolic Profile

## Purpose

This node defines reusable symbolic interpretations that may be attached downstream to the [[Female - Vulva]] expressive route.

It may interpret [[Female - Vulvar Lubrication Activation Profile]], [[Vaginal Fluid]], [[Female - Vulva Sensory Profile]], [[Female - Vulva Somatic Profile]], and [[Female - Vulva Emotional-Motivational Profile]] without redefining any of them.

It is governed by [[Symbolic Meaning Register]].

Symbolic interpretations are optional routes, not intrinsic truths of vulvar anatomy, lubrication, wetness, somatic openness, desire, or any specific person or event.

## Scope Basis

The profile uses Cross-Layer scope because symbolic interpretation may reference anatomy, activation, fluid, sensory, somatic, and motivational records while remaining downstream from all of them.

Cross-Layer scope does not authorize the Symbolic profile to redefine those source layers.

## Biological Boundary

```text
Vulvar anatomy is not symbolic meaning.
Lubrication is not symbolic meaning.
Wetness is not symbolic meaning.
Fluid volume, flow, or surface presence is not symbolic meaning.
```

Symbolic interpretation may be attached downstream but must not be presented as biological fact.

## Sensory / Somatic Boundary

```text
Wetness, texture, warmth, pressure, flow, taste, and odour
= Sensory qualities

Openness, guarding, softening, containment, approach, withdrawal, and release
= Somatic states

Boundary, Threshold, Exposure, Vulnerability, Sovereignty, Visibility, and Self-Recognition
= Symbolic interpretations
```

Shared words such as Containment or Release must remain typed by layer and context.

## Valence / Desire Boundary

Pleasure, discomfort, neutrality, and ambiguity remain valence.

Wanting, non-wanting, ambivalence, aversion, curiosity, indifference, suppression, redirection, and longing remain Emotional / Motivational orientations.

A symbolic interpretation may interpret either, but it does not define or prove them.

## Authorial Boundary

This profile defines reusable symbolic possibilities, not prose, metaphor, cadence, voice, motif frequency, or required narrative language.

Authorial systems decide how a symbolic interpretation is expressed, restrained, transformed, or withheld.

## Corpus Boundary

A work may use one, several, contradictory, or none of these interpretations.

Corpus annotation records situated use. A single story, song, or essay does not redefine this profile as canonical truth.

## Consent Boundary

A symbolic boundary does not imply consent.

## Review Questions

1. Are all proposed terms reusable interpretations rather than anatomy, sensation, somatic state, valence, desire, or authorial phrasing?
2. Does Cross-Layer scope preserve source-layer boundaries rather than collapsing them?
3. Are Boundary and Threshold sufficiently distinct for repeated ontology use?
4. Should Containment and Release remain shared cross-layer terms with explicit typing, or be renamed symbolically?
5. Does Sovereignty remain interpretive without becoming a mandatory ideological reading?
6. Does the profile prevent exposure, visibility, vulnerability, wetness, openness, pleasure, or desire from implying consent?
7. Should [[Symbolic Meaning Register]] eventually move from Meaning Layer or remain the governing bridge into Expressive Layers/Symbolic?

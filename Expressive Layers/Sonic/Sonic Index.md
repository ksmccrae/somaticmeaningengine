# Sonic Index

The Sonic sublayer defines heard, rhythmic, tonal, resonant, musical, acoustic, or vibrational experience.

Sonic records describe sonic experience and pattern. They do not define biological hearing anatomy, activation mechanics, symbolic meaning by default, authorial lineation by default, corpus truth, or consent.

## Governing Documents

- [[Expressive Layer Framework]] — Draft v0.1

## Scope

Sonic records may define experience involving:

```text
sound
rhythm
resonance
hum
vibration
tone
pulse
silence
repetition
pressure-like sound
musical motif
room tone
mechanical sound
voice texture
```

## Boundary Pattern

```text
Sonic
= heard, rhythmic, resonant, tonal, musical, or acoustic experience

Sensory
= perceived qualities, including sound if localized as sensation

Environmental
= atmospheric or spatial context

Symbolic Meaning
= interpretation or significance

Authorial
= language, cadence, and style
```

Sonic profiles may intersect with Sensory and Environmental records but must not redefine them.

# Environmental Index

The Environmental sublayer defines place-based, atmospheric, spatial, seasonal, pressure, material, or weather-related experience.

Environmental records describe experiential context. They do not define objective setting inventory unless governed elsewhere, biological mechanism, activation mechanics, symbolic meaning, authorial style, corpus truth, or consent.

## Governing Documents

- [[Expressive Layer Framework]] — Draft v0.1

## Scope

Environmental records may define experience involving:

```text
weather
barometric pressure
temperature
season
spatial enclosure
material surfaces
light and darkness
room tone
humidity
wind
water
terrain
domestic atmosphere
architectural pressure
```

## Boundary Pattern

```text
Environment
= experiential context or atmospheric condition

Activation
= physiological process or state change

Sensory
= perceived qualities

Symbolic Meaning
= interpretation or significance

Authorial
= language and style
```

Environmental records must not turn atmosphere into unexplained biological mechanism unless an Activation or Translation record governs that mechanism.

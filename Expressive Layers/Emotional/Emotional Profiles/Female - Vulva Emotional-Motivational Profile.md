# Female - Vulva Emotional-Motivational Profile

## Purpose

This node defines affective and motivational orientations that may arise in relation to the [[Female - Vulva]] expressive route.

It may be contextualized by [[Female - Vulvar Lubrication Activation Profile]], informed by [[Female - Vulva Sensory Profile]] and [[Female - Vulva Somatic Profile]], and routed through [[Desire Register]].

It does not define vulvar anatomy, activation mechanics, fluid identity, sensation, somatic body-state, sensory valence, symbolic meaning, authorial language, corpus truth, or consent.

No motivational orientation is inherent to vulvar activation, wetness, lubrication, openness, guarding, pleasure, or any other upstream state.

## Scope Basis

The profile uses Situational / Relational scope because motivational orientation depends on context, object, person, action, memory, intention, or relationship rather than on anatomy alone.

[[Female - Vulva]] provides the embodied context, not the cause or content of desire.

## Activation Boundary

```text
Vulvar activation is not desire.
Lubrication is not desire.
Wetness is not desire.
Fluid flow is not desire.
```

[[Female - Vulvar Lubrication Activation Profile]] may provide physiological context only.

## Sensory Boundary

```text
Sensation
= what is perceived

Motivational orientation
= wanting, non-wanting, ambivalence, aversion, curiosity, indifference, suppression, redirection, or longing
```

Pleasant, unpleasant, intense, neutral, or ambiguous sensation does not determine motivation.

## Somatic Boundary

```text
Somatic approach or withdrawal
= bodily orientation

Motivational wanting or non-wanting
= affective orientation
```

Openness, softening, guarding, bracing, approach, withdrawal, or release may accompany motivation but do not define it.

## Valence Boundary

```text
Pleasure may occur without wanting.
Wanting may occur without pleasure.
Discomfort may occur with wanting or non-wanting.
Neutral valence may occur with any motivational orientation.
Ambivalent valence is not identical to motivational ambivalence.
```

## Symbolic Boundary

Motivational orientation may later receive symbolic interpretation through [[Symbolic Meaning Register]] or a governed Symbolic Profile.

Wanting, non-wanting, aversion, curiosity, or longing does not define symbolic meaning by itself.

## Consent Boundary

```text
Arousal is not consent.
Lubrication is not consent.
Sensation is not consent.
Somatic openness is not consent.
Pleasure is not consent.
Wanting is not consent.
Curiosity is not consent.
Ambivalence is not consent.
Indifference is not consent.
Longing is not consent.
Desire is not consent.
```

Non-wanting and aversion must not be ignored, overridden, or treated as permission.

Consent must be separately and explicitly established.

## Review Questions

1. Is Situational / Relational the correct scope for a motivational profile attached to an embodied route?
2. Are Wanting and Non-Wanting sufficiently distinct from consent and refusal communication?
3. Should Approach and Withdrawal remain only Somatic dimensions while Toward and Away govern motivational direction?
4. Is Ambivalence adequately separated from ambivalent sensory valence?
5. Should Longing, Suppression, and Redirection remain controlled values or become specialized profiles later?
6. Does the profile prevent wetness, openness, pleasure, or desire from being treated as consent?

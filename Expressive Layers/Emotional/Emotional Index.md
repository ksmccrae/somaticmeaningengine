# Emotional Index

The Emotional / Motivational sublayer defines affective and motivational orientation.

It may include desire, refusal, ambivalence, longing, aversion, relief, attachment, fear, grief, tenderness, or other affective orientations when governed.

It does not define activation mechanics, sensory perception, pleasure as valence, symbolic meaning, authorial language, corpus truth, or consent.

## Boundary Pattern

```text
Arousal
= physiological activation state

Sensation
= what is perceived

Pleasure / Valence
= appraisal of perceived sensation

Desire
= motivational or affective orientation

Symbolic Meaning
= interpretation or significance
```

Emotional and motivational records must not infer consent.

# Emotional Index

The Emotional / Motivational sublayer defines affective and motivational orientation.

It may include desire, non-wanting, ambivalence, longing, aversion, curiosity, indifference, suppression, redirection, or other governed orientations.

It does not define activation mechanics, sensory perception, somatic body-state, pleasure as valence, symbolic meaning, authorial language, corpus truth, or consent.

## Boundary Pattern

```text
Arousal
= physiological activation state

Sensation
= what is perceived

Somatic state
= how experience is held or organized in the body

Pleasure / Valence
= appraisal of perceived sensation or body-state

Emotional / Motivational orientation
= wanting, non-wanting, ambivalence, aversion, curiosity, indifference, suppression, redirection, or longing

Symbolic Meaning
= interpretation or significance
```

Emotional and motivational records must not infer consent.

## Somatic / Motivational Boundary

```text
Somatic approach / withdrawal
= bodily orientation

Motivational toward / away
= affective direction
```

The two may align, conflict, or occur independently.

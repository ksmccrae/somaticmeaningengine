# Female - Vulva Somatic Profile

## Purpose

This node defines body-state or embodied-orientation experience associated with [[Female - Vulva]] in contexts where [[Female - Vulvar Lubrication Activation Profile]] may enable or contextualize regional tissue-state and fluid activation, and where [[Female - Vulva Sensory Profile]] may provide localized or regional perception.

It defines somatic experience only. It does not define vulvar anatomy, activation mechanics, fluid identity, fluid production, localized sensory qualities by themselves, sensory valence, pleasure, desire, symbolic meaning, authorial language, corpus usage, diagnosis, or consent.

Wetness, lubrication, or fluid surface presence may inform sensory context, but none of them determines somatic body-state.

## Scope Basis

The profile uses Regional / Whole-Body scope because its primary embodied anchor is [[Female - Vulva]], while the resulting body-state may remain pelvic-centred or echo into wider posture, orientation, guarding, settling, approach, or withdrawal.

The scope does not imply that every referenced anatomical node undergoes a physiological process. Activation mechanics remain governed by [[Female - Vulvar Lubrication Activation Profile]].

6. Does the Consent Boundary correctly preserve ontology separation while ensuring guarding, bracing, and withdrawal are not ignored?

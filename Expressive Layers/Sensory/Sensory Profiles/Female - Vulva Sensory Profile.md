# Female - Vulva Sensory Profile

## Purpose

This node defines perceived sensory qualities associated with [[Female - Vulva]] in contexts where [[Female - Vulvar Lubrication Activation Profile]] may enable or contextualize local tissue-state change, [[Vaginal Fluid]] surface presence, fluid-property alteration, and regional vulvar sensation.

It may reference [[Female - Vulva Fluid Profile]], [[Female - Vaginal Canal Fluid Profile]], [[Vaginal Fluid]], and [[Female - Vulva to Female - Clitoral Complex Mirror Profile]] as upstream or adjacent context.

It defines sensory perception only. It does not define vulvar anatomy, activation mechanics, fluid identity, fluid production, mirror correspondence, sensory valence, pleasure, desire, symbolic meaning, authorial language, corpus usage, or consent.

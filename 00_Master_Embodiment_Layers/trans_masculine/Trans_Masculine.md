# Trans Masculine

**Type:** Master Embodiment Layer (Level 0)  
**Status:** Active

## Purpose

The Trans Masculine layer represents the embodied experience of those who identify and live as masculine, including individuals who have undergone various forms of medical, social, and hormonal transition. It honors both the lived masculine experience and the specific realities of transition.

## Core Dimensions

### Anatomy & Physiology
- Post-surgical anatomy (top surgery, hysterectomy, phalloplasty, metoidioplasty, etc.)
- Hormone replacement therapy (testosterone)
- Facial and body hair growth from HRT
- Altered chest, genital, and pelvic sensation
- Voice changes and muscle mass shifts

### Hormonal & Reproductive States
- Testosterone-dominant hormonal profiles (exogenous)
- Cessation of menstruation (usually)
- Changes in libido, genital sensitivity, and orgasm
- Potential effects on reproductive organs

### Embodied Experience
- Chest and nipple sensation post top surgery
- Genital growth and changed sensation (clitoral growth, etc.)
- Facial hair and body hair development
- Shift in strength, voice, and physical presence
- Gender euphoria and dysphoria as somatic experiences

## Shared Elements

Elements that overlap with other embodiment layers:

- Skin, breath, temperature, touch, fatigue, pain, hunger, ageing, mortality
- Emotional and nervous system responses

## Specific Considerations

Unique aspects of this embodiment layer:

- Embodied experience of medical transition and its ongoing effects
- Navigation of both assigned and affirmed embodiment
- Specific post-surgical and HRT-related sensations
- Often carries complex relationships with "male" bodily expectations and cultural meanings

## Relationship to Tropes

How this embodiment layer typically interacts with or selects tropes:

- Tropes involving transition, medicalization, or transformation often engage this layer directly
- Testosterone-related changes (voice, hair, libido, strength) frequently interact with tropes
- Some tropes take on additional layers around authenticity, passing, or medical intervention

## Mirror Dynamics

How this layer generates or receives mirror responses:

- HRT and post-surgical states create unique mirror patterns
- Changes in voice, hair, and physical presence can produce strong resonance
- Gendered bodily experiences create complex mirror dynamics with both cis and other trans embodiments

## Notes

- This layer must be handled with care, respect, and precision
- Avoid reducing trans masculine experience to medical transition alone
- Honor both the shared masculine experience and the distinct realities of transition

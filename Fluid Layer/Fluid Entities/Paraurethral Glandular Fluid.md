## Purpose

This node defines Paraurethral Glandular Fluid as a reusable fluid entity in the Somatic Meaning Engine.

## Canonical Definition

Paraurethral Glandular Fluid is glandular fluid associated with paraurethral glands, including [[Female - Skene's Glands]] where relevant anatomy is present.

It may be referenced by anatomical fluid profiles involving paraurethral glands, vestibular or paraurethral regions, nearby gland openings, urethral-adjacent structures, or surface presence near the vestibule.

It should remain distinct from [[Urine]], [[Vaginal Fluid]], [[Cervical Mucus]], and [[Menstrual Fluid]].

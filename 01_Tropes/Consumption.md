# Consumption

**Type:** Trope (Level 0)  
**Function:** Selection & Activation Logic

## Purpose

The Consumption trope centers on being consumed, devoured, used up, or taken into another. It often carries themes of surrender, sacrifice, nourishment, and the erotic or existential merging of bodies.

## Activation Logic

What this trope tends to emphasize or bring forward:

- Embodiment pathways: Being eaten, swallowed, absorbed, drained, or metabolized
- Relationship structures: Predator/prey, consumer/consumed, feeder/feeder, devourer/devoured
- Expressive registers: Surrender, fear, ecstasy, dissolution, and erotic annihilation

## Relational Dynamics

Common pair/power/witness configurations associated with this trope:

- Predator / Prey
- Devourer / Devoured
- Feeder / Consumed
- Consumer / Consumed

## Propagation Patterns

Typical ways this trope moves through systems:

- Capture → consumption → dissolution or transformation → absorption into the other
- Fear → surrender → erotic merging

## Mirror Effects

Common sympathetic or cross-body responses associated with this trope:

- Consumption often produces intense mirror responses around surrender and fear
- Being devoured can create strong identification in witnesses
- The theme of merging or being taken into another resonates widely

## Notes / Constraints

- This trope requires careful handling of consent and aftercare
- Strong potential for both terror and erotic transcendence
- Works best when grounded in sensation and emotional truth

- Vore and unbirth scenes

- Sacrifice and offering through consumption

# Femdom

**Type:** Trope (Level 0)  
**Function:** Selection & Activation Logic

## Purpose

The Femdom trope centers on female-dominant power exchange. It often involves control, service, worship, discipline, and the erotic charge of feminine authority and masculine (or submissive) surrender.

## Activation Logic

What this trope tends to emphasize or bring forward:

- Embodiment pathways: Posture, voice, touch, command, restraint, denial, and service
- Relationship structures: Dominant/submissive, owner/pet, queen/subject, mistress/servant
- Expressive registers: Authority, control, devotion, surrender, teasing, and ritualized power

## Common Embodiment Interactions

| Embodiment Layer   | Typical Expression / Emphasis                                      |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Female             | Dominant women, matriarchal dynamics, feminine authority           |
| Male               | Male submission to female authority                                |
| Trans Feminine     | Dominant trans women, feminine authority after transition          |
| Trans Masculine    | Submission from trans masculine bodies                             |
| Shared             | General power exchange dynamics                                    |

## Relational Dynamics

Common pair/power/witness configurations associated with this trope:

- Mistress / Submissive
- Owner / Pet
- Queen / Subject
- Dominant woman / Submissive partner

## Propagation Patterns

Typical ways this trope moves through systems:

- Command → obedience or resistance → punishment or reward → deepening surrender
- Service → devotion → identity shift

## Mirror Effects

Common sympathetic or cross-body responses associated with this trope:

- Female authority often produces strong mirror responses in submissives
- Posture, voice, and command can create resonant power dynamics
- Surrender and service frequently mirror across embodiments

## Notes / Constraints

- This trope benefits from clear negotiation and aftercare
- Works well when feminine authority is embodied rather than performed
- Can easily become caricature if not grounded in real power exchange

## Example Usage

- Female-led relationships and domestic discipline
- Service submission and worship
- Tease and denial dynamics
- Ritualized power exchange scenes

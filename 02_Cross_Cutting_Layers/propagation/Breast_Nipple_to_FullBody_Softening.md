**Primary Embodiment:** Female

## Description

**Stage 1 – Breast / Nipple Activation**
- Touch, temperature, pressure, or sucking on breasts/nipples
- Hormonal or arousal response triggered

**Stage 2 – Chest & Core Release**
- Chest and shoulder softening
- Breath deepens

**Stage 3 – Full-Body & Emotional Softening**

- Often accompanied by nurturing or receptive states
- Can lead to deep relaxation or trance-like states

- Potential for deep rest or bonding states
- Frequently seen in nursing, breast worship, and gentle intimacy

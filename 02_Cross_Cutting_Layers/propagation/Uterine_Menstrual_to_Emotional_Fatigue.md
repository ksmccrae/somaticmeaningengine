# Uterine / Menstrual → Emotional & Fatigue (Internal Propagation)

**Type:** Internal Propagation  
**Primary Embodiment:** Female

## Description

This propagation describes how uterine and menstrual states move into emotional sensitivity and physical/emotional fatigue.

## Propagation Path

**Stage 1 – Uterine / Menstrual Activation**
- Cramps, contractions, or hormonal shifts
- Blood and fluid awareness

**Stage 2 – Systemic Spread**
- Lower back, pelvic, and thigh tension
- Hormonal influence on nervous system

**Stage 3 – Emotional & Fatigue Response**
- Increased emotional sensitivity or irritability
- Physical and emotional fatigue
- Desire for withdrawal or rest
- Possible emotional lability or introspection

## Typical Outcomes

- Need for rest and containment
- Heightened emotional reactivity
- Cyclical patterns of energy and withdrawal
- Potential for deep emotional processing

## Notes

- Strongly time-based and cyclical
- One of the clearest examples of hormonal propagation into emotional and energetic states
- Works well in scenes involving menstruation, hormonal intensity, or cyclical surrender
